About

I'm a fifth-semester Actuarial Science undergraduate at Universitas Negeri Surabaya (UNESA), expected to graduate in January 2028.

My current research applies extreme value theory and copula models to climate extremes. In UNESA's 2026 Academic Research Mobility Program I fitted GEV distributions to seasonal temperature maxima from seven West Java stations and compared a Gaussian copula with a Dynamic Copula Bayesian Network. I wrote the manuscript as first author, and it is now under peer review.

I work mainly in Python and R, with Excel, SPSS, and LaTeX alongside. I plan to sit the Society of Actuaries of Indonesia (PAI) exams, and I'm looking for internships in life and general insurance, pension funds, or risk analysis and financial system stability.
